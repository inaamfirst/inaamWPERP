# Product Vision

Enterprise Commerce ERP is a commercial Windows-first ERP product for retail,
wholesale, distribution, and future marketplace businesses.

The desktop application is the control center. WooCommerce is the online
storefront. The ERP is the business brain that owns internal operations,
permissions, audit, stock, sync policy, and commercial licensing.

## Target Businesses

- Mobile stores
- Cosmetics shops
- Electronics stores
- Grocery and super stores
- Boutiques
- Wholesale businesses
- Distributors
- Future multi-vendor marketplaces

The product must stay configurable and must not hardcode a single business type.

## Product Boundaries

The current foundation includes tenant-scoped identity, catalog, customers,
inventory, orders, reports, WooCommerce sync foundation, WhatsApp notification
foundation, backup/licensing foundations, desktop shell screens, and packaging
operations assets.

It is still not the full ERP product. Accounting, delivery/COD, marketplace,
mobile apps, AI, signed installer/update delivery, real WhatsApp sending, and
live WooCommerce rollout certification remain future work.

AI is a future premium module. It may assist with support, reports, analytics,
and suggestions, but it must not automatically place customer orders without
human confirmation.
